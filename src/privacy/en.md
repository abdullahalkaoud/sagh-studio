## 2. What we collect

**a. If you fill in the project brief (sagh.co/en/brief):** your name, company or project name, WhatsApp number, email (optional), city, project details, budget range, expected timeline, and any reference links you add.

**b. If you contact us on WhatsApp:** your number, name and whatever you write in the message.

**c. When you browse the site, without consent:** our host (Cloudflare) processes technical data needed to deliver and protect the site, such as IP address, browser type and request time. We also store your cookie-bar choice in your browser so we do not ask every time.

**d. When you browse the site, only if you press "Accept" in the cookie bar:** we run Google Analytics to measure how the site is used. It collects:
- a random identifier stored in a cookie in your browser;
- the pages you open, visit duration and where you came from;
- your presses on specific buttons, such as the WhatsApp button and starting or sending the brief;
- your device, browser and language;
- your approximate location (city or region) derived from your IP address. Google states that Google Analytics does not log or store IP addresses.

We do not send Google Analytics your name, number, email or brief answers.

If you press "Decline" or make no choice, Google Analytics does not run, no cookie is set for it, and nothing is sent to it. The site works the same for you.

We use no advertising cookies or ad-tracking tools, and we do not show you ads based on your visit.

Please do not put sensitive data in the brief (health information, personal financial details, ID numbers). We do not need it.

## 3. Why we use it
- **Brief and WhatsApp:** to understand your project, prepare a quote and contact you about it. If you become a client, to deliver the project under our agreement.
- **Analytics:** to see which pages help visitors and which need work, in aggregate numbers.

We do not sell your data. We do not send you promotional messages on WhatsApp or email without your separate consent.

## 4. Legal basis
- **Brief and WhatsApp:** your **consent**, given by ticking the consent box in the brief and by sending the message yourself (Personal Data Protection Law, Article 5). If you become a client, processing is done to perform the agreement you are a party to (Article 6).
- **Analytics cookies:** your **consent** in the cookie bar (Article 5).
- **Hosting technical data and storing your cookie-bar choice:** needed to run and protect the site and to apply your choice.

Consent to analytics is optional. It is not a condition for browsing the site or asking for a quote. Providing brief data is optional, but without your name, WhatsApp number and project details we cannot prepare a quote.

## 5. Where it goes and who receives it

**Brief and WhatsApp data**
- **Google Sheets (Google):** as soon as you press "Send on WhatsApp", all your answers are saved automatically to Sagh's follow-up sheet on Google Sheets, even if you do not send the WhatsApp message.
- **WhatsApp (Meta):** in our chats with you on Sagh's phone.
- **Sagh's device and work tools:** we keep a copy of your request on the establishment's device, and we use an AI assistant (Claude, by Anthropic) that reads your request and helps us prepare your project concept.

**Browsing data**
- **Cloudflare:** hosting and protection, for all visitors.
- **Google (Google Tag Manager and Google Analytics):** only after you accept; Google processes analytics data on Sagh's behalf.

**Transfer outside Saudi Arabia:** the servers of Google, Meta, Anthropic and Cloudflare are, or may be, outside the Kingdom, so your data is transferred and processed there. We transfer the minimum needed and we do not ask you for sensitive data.

Access to your data on our side is limited to the owner. We do not share it with anyone else unless a law obliges us or a competent authority requests it.

## 6. How long we keep it
- **Brief and WhatsApp, if no project starts:** deleted from the sheet, our device and our WhatsApp chats **12 months** after our last contact, or sooner if you ask.
- **If you become a client:** for the project, and then for the period the law obliges us to keep it.
- **Google Analytics:** visitor and event data is deleted from our account automatically after **2 months**. Analytics cookies stay in your browser for up to 2 years, or until you clear them.
- **Your cookie-bar choice:** stored in your browser for **180 days**, then we ask again.

## 7. How to withdraw consent
- **Analytics:** press "Cookie settings" at the bottom of any page and choose "Decline". One press, the same as accepting. From then on Google Analytics stops in your browser; you can clear cookies set earlier in your browser settings.
- **Brief and WhatsApp:** send us "I withdraw my consent" or "please delete my data" on WhatsApp at +966558000321.

Withdrawing consent does not affect what was done before.

## 8. Your rights
You have the right to:
- know what we collect, why, and on what legal basis;
- get a copy of your data;
- correct or update it;
- ask us to delete it;
- withdraw consent at any time.

**How:** message us on WhatsApp at +966558000321. We reply within **30 days** at most; if we need more time we will tell you why before then.
Analytics data is tied to an identifier in your browser, not your name, so we can only link it to you if you give us that identifier.
If you are not satisfied with our reply, you can complain to the Saudi Data & AI Authority (SDAIA).

## 9. Security
We protect the phone, the device and the WhatsApp and Google accounts with a device lock and two-step verification, limit access to the owner, and collect the minimum. If a breach is likely to harm you, we will notify you and the competent authority as the law requires.

## 10. Children
Our services are for adults and businesses, and the brief is for people aged 18 or older. If we learn that someone younger sent us data, we delete it.
